Title: Shift-Start Department Emergency Access

MODULE ID: SHIFT_START_EMERGENCY_ACCESS

Description:

Gives departments a grace period at the start of a shift to fill their jobs. After the delay, the system finds station airlocks whose access requirements match an unstaffed department, announces the affected departments, and enables emergency access on those airlocks. Cargo's supply shuttle doors are included. When a player later spawns into a department, the system revokes emergency access from airlocks it enabled for that department. Airlocks already in emergency mode before the system's scan are left unchanged.

Engineering, Medical, Science, Cargo, and Service are included. Command and Security are excluded. Matching uses required access flags rather than area location. Unrestricted doors, doors with command access, and doors with ACCESS_MAINT_TUNNELS in req_one_access are ignored. Maintenance access in req_access does not by itself exclude a door.
