DeactivateDeactivates bite mode, restoring normal unarmed attacks.
DestroyCleanup proc - ensures bite mode is deactivated before the action is destroyed

proc
add_bite_bonuses
(/obj/item/bodypart/head/head)

Applies bite bonuses to the head, matching the bonuses a baseline fang organ grants. These bonuses are: +4/+7 damage, +10 effectiveness, +0.5 pummeling, and sharpness.

Arguments:

  • head - The head bodypart to apply bonuses to

proc
check_removed_organ
(/mob/living/carbon/human/loser, /obj/item/organ/organ_lost, special)

Signal handler for when an organ is removed from the mob. If a fang organ is removed while bite mode is active, apply our bonuses (since the fangs are no longer providing them) and switch to listening for organ gain.

Arguments:

  • loser - The mob that lost the organ
  • organ_lost - The organ that was removed

proc
remove_bite_bonuses
