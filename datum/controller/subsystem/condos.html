Procs

create_roomBuilds a brand new room from a template: reserves space, loads it, registers a /datum/condo_room and warps the owner in. Returns the room (or null on failure).
link_condo_turfsPoints the room's /area/ and door at the room datum.
load_or_render_previewReturns an interior's preview icon: from the on-disk cache if the .dmm is unchanged (no map loading at all), otherwise renders it fresh and caches it. The map hash in the filename is the cache key, so editing a map automatically invalidates its cached picture.
photograph_interiorLoads one interior into a temp reservation, flattens it to an icon, frees the room.
preload_condo_templatesRegisters every /datum/map_template/condo subtype as a pickable interior.
prerender_previewsPhotographs every interior once and caches the pictures as assets. Runs in the background at round start so it never blocks anything.
warp_into_roomDrops a mob at a room's landing spot.
