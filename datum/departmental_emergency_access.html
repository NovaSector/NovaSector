departmental_emergency_access
/datum/departmental_emergency_access

Gives departments time to fill their jobs at round start, before making them accessible. Five minutes after round start, airlocks associated with unstaffed departments get emergency access and the affected departments are announced. When a player spawns into a department, this system revokes emergency access from that department. Command and Security are excluded. (for obvious reasons).
